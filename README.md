# Philippine Energy Supply and Consumption Analysis

## Project Overview

The Philippine Energy Supply and Consumption Analysis is a data visualization project developed using Tableau. It explores energy consumption across different sectors in the Philippines and examines yearly trends in energy consumption and acquisition from 2014 to 2022.

The dashboard presents interactive visualizations that allow users to examine annual energy consumption trends, compare consumption across sectors, and explore energy acquisition by source.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze yearly energy consumption trends.
- Identify sectors with the highest and lowest energy consumption.
- Compare energy consumption across different sectors.
- Examine yearly energy acquisition trends.
- Identify the leading energy source in the observed data.
- Present energy-related findings through interactive visualizations.

## Tools and Technologies

- Tableau
- Energy Supply and Consumption Dataset

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes:

- Total Energy Consumption per Year
- Total Energy Consumption by Sector
- Sector Consumption per Year
- Total Energy Acquired per Year
- Energy Acquisition by Source

Interactive filters allow users to explore the data by sector, resource, year, and energy source.

## Key Findings and Observations

1. **Annual Energy Consumption Trend:** Energy consumption generally increased from 2014 to 2019, declined in 2020, and increased again through 2022.
2. **Transportation Sector:** Transportation recorded the highest energy consumption among the sectors presented.
3. **Agriculture Sector:** Agriculture recorded the lowest energy consumption among the sectors presented.
4. **Energy Acquisition:** 2022 recorded the highest level of energy acquisition during the observed period.
5. **Geothermal Energy:** Geothermal recorded the highest energy acquisition among the energy sources presented.

## Recommendations and Implications

- Promote energy efficiency and sustainable transportation initiatives.
- Explore opportunities to improve energy efficiency in the Agriculture sector.
- Monitor yearly changes in energy consumption.
- Examine the factors contributing to increased energy acquisition in 2022.
- Recognize the contribution of geothermal energy to the country's energy supply.
